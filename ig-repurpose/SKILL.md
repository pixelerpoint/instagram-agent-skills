# /ig-repurpose - one idea, a week of content

## Account context (@warmnode)
- Bio: "Rent a friend for coffee & deep talks." Voice: warm, calm, honest. Audience: 18-30 overthinkers.
- The owner has almost no time. Repurposing is how they post more without thinking of more ideas.

## Steps
1. Take the input (a caption, a carousel's text, a reel script, or an idea).
2. Produce, each copy-ready:
   - **Carousel version:** slide-by-slide text (then offer to render it with /ig-carousel).
   - **Reel version:** hook + 3-4 timed text beats + b-roll idea (format from /ig-reel).
   - **Story set:** 3 frames, one with a poll or question sticker.
   - **Threads post:** 1-3 short lines, conversational, ending in a question. (Threads is linked to Instagram and is easy reach for text content.)
   - **TikTok / YouTube Shorts:** same video as the reel; give a platform caption and 3-5 hashtags for each. TikTok captions can be more casual; YouTube needs a searchable title (e.g. "Deep questions to ask your best friend").
   - **Facebook:** a slightly longer caption for the same reel or carousel.
3. Suggest a spread: post the original, then the repurposed versions across the next 3-10 days so it doesn't feel repeated.
4. Note: when cross-posting a reel to TikTok/Shorts, upload the clean video without the Instagram watermark.

## Output
Clearly labelled blocks per platform. No commentary.
