# /ig-dm - messages people actually answer

## Account context (@warmnode)
- Bio: "Rent a friend for coffee & deep talks." Voice: warm, calm, honest. Mirror the other person's language (English, Urdu, Roman Urdu).
- DMs and shares are among the strongest signals Instagram uses, and real conversations are the whole point of this account.

## Rules
- The user sends every DM themselves. No mass or automated DMs: Instagram limits accounts that do this, and it kills trust.
- Short (2-4 lines), personal, one clear ask or none at all.
- Never copy-paste the same opener to many people; personalise one detail every time.
- Keep it friendly and platonic. Never anything that could read as flirting or pressure, unless the user explicitly says that's the context.

## Message types
1. **New follower welcome:** thank them, one warm line, one easy question ("what made you follow, the deep talks or the coffee? ☕").
2. **Someone engaged (liked, commented, replied to a story):** pick up exactly what they said and keep it going.
3. **Collab pitch** (to an account of similar size in the niche): compliment one specific post, propose one concrete idea (a joint carousel, a shared "deep questions" series, a story shoutout swap), make it easy to say yes.
4. **Follow-up 1** (after 3-4 days of no reply): one light line, no guilt.
5. **Follow-up 2** (a week later): close the loop kindly. Then stop.

## Steps
1. Ask nothing unless you truly don't know who the message is for. Use whatever the user gives (handle, post, situation).
2. Write the message. For collab pitches, also write both follow-ups.
3. Give 2 versions when the stakes are higher (collabs), mark your pick.

## Output
Copy-ready messages only.
