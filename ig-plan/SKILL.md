# /ig-plan - a week that runs on 30 minutes of batching

## Account context (@warmnode)
- Bio: "Rent a friend for coffee & deep talks." Voice: warm, calm, honest.
- Audience: 18-30 overthinkers who crave real conversation. Based in Pakistan (PKT, UTC+5).
- Pillars: deep questions, quiet truths about loneliness/friendship, coffee-table confessions, being a better friend/listener.
- The owner has almost no time. The plan must fit one 30-minute batch session per week plus ~10-15 minutes a day.
- If the user says their niche or schedule changed, follow what they say now.

## Default rhythm (adjust if their insights say otherwise)
- **Posts:** 4 per week. 2 carousels + 2 text reels. Faceless.
- **Stories:** 2-3 frames on 4-5 days (use /ig-story).
- **Times:** 8-10 pm PKT weekdays, 1-3 pm or 9-11 pm weekends.
- **Daily 10-15 min:** reply to every comment and DM (/ig-reply, /ig-dm), then leave 5-10 thoughtful comments on bigger accounts in the niche (/ig-comment). Engagement in the first hour after posting matters most.
- **New account:** for the first 2 weeks, aim to get to 9 posts so the profile looks alive, then settle into the rhythm.

## Steps
1. Make a 7-day table: Day | Time | Format | Topic/hook | Pillar | Story idea.
2. Write the actual hook for each post (not "a post about friendship").
3. Add the **batch session** checklist: in one sitting, run /ig-carousel twice and /ig-reel twice, download everything, and schedule posts in Instagram's own scheduler (the "..." / advanced settings when posting lets you schedule ahead), so the week posts itself.
4. List 5-10 types of accounts to engage with this week (e.g. café pages, psychology/quote pages, mental-health creators, friendship meme pages) with search terms to find them.
5. One weekly check-in: which post did best and what to repeat.

## Output
The table, then the batch checklist, then the engagement list. Short.
