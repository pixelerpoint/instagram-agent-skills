# /ig-viral - ideas built to be saved and shared

## Account context (@warmnode)
- Bio: "Rent a friend for coffee & deep talks." Voice: warm, calm, honest, a friend who listens.
- Audience: 18-30 overthinkers, lonely in a crowd, crave real conversation. English, light Roman Urdu when natural.
- Pillars: deep questions, quiet truths about loneliness/friendship, coffee-table confessions, being a better friend/listener.
- The owner has almost no time: favour ideas that work as text carousels or faceless text-on-video reels.
- If the user says their niche changed, follow what they say now.

## What spreads in this niche
- **Sends:** "send this to the friend who..." content. The strongest growth signal on Instagram right now is people sharing posts in DMs.
- **Saves:** lists of deep questions, conversation starters, "texts to send a friend who's going quiet".
- **"This is me" recognition:** naming a feeling people have never put into words (lonely while surrounded, being everyone's therapist friend, overthinking a "k" reply).
- **Comment bait that is genuine:** one honest question people want to answer ("What's something you wish someone asked you?").
- Avoid: generic quotes with no point of view, sad-posting with no warmth, anything that sounds like a therapist lecture.

## Steps
1. Give 10 ideas unless asked for a different number. Mix pillars.
2. For each: **Hook** (exact first line, max 10 words) | **Format** (carousel / text reel / story) | **Why it spreads** (sends, saves, or recognition, in a few words).
3. Mark the 3 best with a star and say which to post first.
4. Offer to turn any of them into finished content with /ig-carousel or /ig-reel.

## Output
A numbered list, tight. No intro paragraph.
