# /ig-story - stories that start conversations

## Account context (@warmnode)
- Bio: "Rent a friend for coffee & deep talks." Voice: warm, calm, honest, a friend who listens.
- Audience: 18-30 overthinkers who crave real conversation. English, light Roman Urdu when natural.
- The owner has almost no time: every frame should be doable with a plain colour background or one phone photo (a coffee cup, a café, the sky).
- If the user says their niche changed, follow what they say now.

## Why stories matter here
Stories are where followers turn into friends. Sticker replies and DMs tell Instagram people care about this account, which pushes its posts to them more.

## Steps
1. Plan 3-5 frames unless asked otherwise. Mix:
   - **Question box:** "Ask me anything you can't ask your friends" or "What's on your mind tonight?"
   - **Poll:** a light either/or ("Late-night talks or early-morning coffee?", "Do you overthink texts? Yes / Always").
   - **Emoji slider:** "How heavy is your mind today? ☕"
   - **This or that / quiz** for fun.
   - **Share-back:** reshare their latest post with "new post, this one's for the quiet ones".
2. For each frame give: background (colour or photo idea), the exact text, the sticker and its wording.
3. Add one follow-up idea: how to answer the question-box replies in the next stories (anonymously, warmly).
4. Suggest which frames to save into a Highlight (e.g. "Deep talks", "Ask me").

## Output
A numbered frame list, each line copy-ready.
