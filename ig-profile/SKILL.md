# /ig-profile - turn visitors into followers

## Account context (@warmnode)
- Handle: @warmnode. Current bio: "Rent a friend for coffee & deep talks." Name field: "Warmnode".
- Voice: warm, calm, honest. Audience: 18-30 overthinkers who crave real conversation.
- If the user shares a screenshot or new details, use those; they override this block.

## Rubric (score each, total 100)
| # | Part | Pts | What full marks looks like |
|---|------|-----|----------------------------|
| 1 | Profile photo | 8 | Clear, warm, recognisable at tiny size; face or one simple symbol |
| 2 | Name field | 10 | Contains a searchable keyword, e.g. "Warmnode · Deep Talks" (the name field is searchable, the bio is less so) |
| 3 | Bio line 1 | 12 | Says who it's for and what they get, in plain words |
| 4 | Bio line 2 | 8 | A reason to follow: what you post / how often |
| 5 | Call to action | 8 | Tells people what to do: "DM me 'coffee' to talk" or "new deep question every day" |
| 6 | Link | 6 | Present and useful (a free resource, a form, a linktree) or intentionally absent |
| 7 | Highlights | 8 | 3-4 clear ones: Start here / Deep talks / Ask me / About |
| 8 | Pinned posts | 10 | 3 pinned: one that explains the account, one best performer, one community post |
| 9 | Grid consistency | 10 | Covers look like they belong together (same colours, fonts) |
| 10 | Post count | 8 | At least 9 posts before pushing growth hard |
| 11 | Niche clarity | 8 | A stranger knows in 3 seconds what this account is |
| 12 | Trust/safety | 4 | "Rent a friend" framing is clear that it's about conversation and kindness, nothing that reads as dating or paid services unless intended |

## Steps
1. Score the profile from the screenshot or details given. Show the table with points earned.
2. Rewrite every part below full marks. For the bio, give 3 options (max 150 characters, line breaks included) and mark your pick.
3. Give the top 3 fixes in order of impact, each doable in under 2 minutes.

## Output
Score table, rewrites, top 3 fixes. Copy-ready text in blocks.
