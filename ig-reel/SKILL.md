# /ig-reel - a reel you can film in 5 minutes

## Account context (@warmnode)
- Bio: "Rent a friend for coffee & deep talks." Voice: a warm, calm friend across the coffee table. Honest, gentle, never preachy.
- Audience: 18-30 overthinkers who feel lonely in a crowd and crave real conversation. English, light Roman Urdu when natural.
- Pillars: deep questions, quiet truths about loneliness/friendship, coffee-table confessions, being a better friend/listener.
- The owner has almost no time and may not want to show their face. Default to faceless text-on-video reels.
- If the user says their niche changed, follow what they say now.

## Steps
1. No topic given? Pick one from the pillars yourself. Don't ask.
2. Choose a format, defaulting to the cheapest:
   - **Text-on-b-roll (default):** 7-12 seconds, one clip (coffee being poured, a café window, rain, hands around a cup, an empty chair), 2-4 text overlays. Ideal for no time.
   - **"POV" or "Things a real friend does" list:** 15-20 seconds, 4-6 quick text beats over 2-3 clips.
   - **Voiceover:** only if the user says they're fine recording their voice.
3. Write:
   - **Hook (0-3s):** the first on-screen line. Max 8 words. A feeling they recognise or a question they can't ignore. E.g. "If nobody asked you today: how are you, really?"
   - **On-screen text beats** with timing (0-3s, 3-6s ...). Max 10 words per beat, large readable text.
   - **Shot list:** exactly what to film or where to get it free (their own phone in a café, or free stock from Pexels/Pixabay). One line each.
   - **Audio:** describe the mood (soft lo-fi, slow piano) and tell them to pick a trending sound with that mood in Instagram's audio library. Don't name copyrighted songs as required.
   - **Caption + hashtags** following /ig-caption rules.
   - **Cover text** for the grid: 3-6 words.
4. Design for rewatches: short reels that loop cleanly and end on a line that makes people read again.

## Output
Hook, timed text beats, shot list, audio, caption block, cover text. Copy-ready, no extra commentary.
